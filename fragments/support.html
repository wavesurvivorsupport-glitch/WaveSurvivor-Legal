Support

We can help.

For bugs, technical issues or questions about WaveSurvivor, email Haider Games directly. There is no website form or account to create.

What to include

Help us trace it.

A few details make support faster. Share only what you are comfortable sharing.

Technical problems

Tell us your Windows version, what you were doing and the exact error if one appeared.

Bug reports

Include steps to reproduce the issue, whether it repeats, and the game mode and role involved.

Multiplayer issues

Say whether you were hosting or joining, the player count and what happened to the session.

Steam and platform

For purchases or refunds, use Steam Support. For game-specific platform issues, contact us.

Direct contact

Send us a message.

wavesurvivor.support@gmail.com

Please avoid sending passwords, account credentials or other sensitive information.
