Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Haider Games develops WaveSurvivor. This policy explains the information processed when you play the current Windows PC game, especially when you use online co-op. Platform providers also process information under their own policies. The game does not require a separate Haider Games account.

1. Information processed

Information kept on your device

WaveSurvivor stores offline Story and Endless save slots on your computer. These can contain a save name, role, character, chapter or stage, wave, level, experience, health, armor, chosen upgrades, and Story progress. A local profile records lifetime enemy defeats. Game settings and key bindings are stored locally. For online reconnect, the game may temporarily save a session identifier and mode in local preferences. This game code does not upload these save files to a Haider Games server. Your platform or computer backup settings may handle local files separately.

Online co-op

Online play uses a Unity-generated anonymous player ID and session credentials. You can choose a display name and, when creating a room, a room name and optional password. Unity's session services process player and room identifiers, names, host status, selected role, ready state, appearance choices, party occupancy, mode, and run status such as wave or level. The game sends the information needed to create, find, join, and maintain a session and to reconnect. During a match, gameplay and connection data pass between players through Unity Relay and Netcode for GameObjects. Other players in your room can see your in-game display name and relevant lobby or gameplay state.

Anonymous Unity sign-in is for online services; it is not a Haider Games email or password account. The project contains no separate Haider Games player database. Haider Games may be able to view some service records and session metadata through its Unity developer account.

Steam and Discord

If a Steamworks runtime is available, WaveSurvivor may read your Steam persona name for the Main Menu greeting. The inspected game code does not use a Steam ID as its online session identity or send that persona name to Haider Games. Steam separately processes account, purchase, game activity, and platform data under its own policy.

On supported desktop systems, WaveSurvivor sends Discord Rich Presence through the official Discord Social SDK to your running Discord client. The activity can show the game, mode, role, Story objective or Endless wave, online party size, artwork, and elapsed session time. The game does not request your Discord account profile or friends, link a Discord account, or use Discord chat or voice. Discord controls who can see your activity through your Discord settings.

Technical information and contact

The game writes local diagnostic messages for operation and errors. No dedicated in-game analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting integration was identified in the current project. Steam, Unity, Discord, and the host of these web pages may independently process technical data such as connection, device, and service logs under their own policies. If you contact Haider Games, we will receive the information you choose to provide in that request.

2. Third-party services

  • Valve / Steam distributes and operates the Steam platform. See Steam's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Gaming Services provides anonymous authentication, multiplayer sessions, and Relay. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Discord receives Rich Presence through the desktop client when available. See Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Website host may process ordinary web request information to deliver these pages. This static site itself sets no cookies and includes no analytics, advertising, or external JavaScript.

3. Why information is used and shared

The information above is used to save your offline progress and preferences; create, operate, and recover online rooms and matches; display a Steam persona name when available; show optional Discord game activity; and respond to support or privacy requests. Multiplayer information is shared with Unity services and the players in your session as needed for online play. Rich Presence is sent to Discord when its desktop client is available. Haider Games does not sell personal information and has not integrated advertising into the game.

4. Retention and security

Local saves and settings remain on your device until you remove them or the game or platform removes them. The reconnect preference is cleared by the game when the related session is no longer needed. Unity, Steam, Discord, and the website host set retention periods for data they process on their services; consult their policies for details. Haider Games does not operate a separate persistent player-profile server for this game. Information you send to support is kept only as reasonably needed to answer the request and meet applicable obligations.

We use the security controls available through the game and service providers, but no online service or local storage is perfectly secure. Do not put sensitive personal information in a player name or room name.

5. Your choices and rights

You can play offline without creating a Unity multiplayer session. You can edit or delete local save slots and change game settings in the game; removing the game's local data may also remove saves and preferences. Discord activity visibility is controlled in Discord. Steam, Unity, and Discord offer their own account and privacy controls for information they hold.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information, or to object to processing. Contact Haider Games for information under our control. We may need enough details to locate a relevant Unity session or support record and verify your request. For Steam or Discord account data, contact those providers directly. We will respond as required by applicable law.

6. Children and international use

WaveSurvivor is not directed to children under 13. Players must meet the age requirements of Steam and other services they use, and any higher age required by local law. If you believe a child has given Haider Games personal information, contact us. Online providers may process data in countries other than yours, subject to their own policies and safeguards.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The date above shows the latest revision. Material changes will be made available through an appropriate game or platform notice where reasonably possible.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Haider Games at wavesurvivor.support@gmail.com.
