v0.1.37 — Offline Story Spawn Regions

Offline Story enemies and Healer Hearts now use the authored spawn areas for each Core location, with improved Boss arena add placement.

Story

  • Explore and Core defense enemies now spawn from authored region volumes, with separate spawn groups for each Blue and Green Core location.
  • Healer Hearts now appear within the authored Heart areas for the active Story region.

Boss

  • Boss1 begins with a small protective pack; later adds enter from the full Blue spawn pool and can pass through the arena blockers while players and Boss1 remain contained.
