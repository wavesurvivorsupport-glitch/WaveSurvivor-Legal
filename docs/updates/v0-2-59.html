v0.2.59 - PracticeMachine launch feel polish

A larger initial interaction area and fast-launch, slower-descent timing along the existing high arcs.

PracticeMachine prototype

  • PRACTICE MACHINE: Initial interaction footprint expands from 2x2 to 3x3 meters, preserving its center, height, vertical tolerance, interaction logic and the later Practice Position.
  • TARGET MOTION: Targets launch faster and ease into a slower final half using a smooth distance-based clock. Equal quarters of the unchanged arc take 0.450, 0.600, 0.975 and 0.975 seconds, totaling exactly three seconds.
  • PRESERVED: Single/double/triple rises remain 10m, 11m each and 12/10/12m. Endpoints, hit registration, scoring, target counts, 2.5-second initial delay, all-targets-resolved gating and one-second next-group wait remain unchanged. Thrower motion and combat balance are untouched.
