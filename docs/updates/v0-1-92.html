v0.1.92 - District 2 Practice guidance and pacing

Clear interaction and stand guidance, full objective text, faster resolution-based target cadence and separated launch origins.

Practice Machine playtest polish

  • IMPROVED: Practice Machine guidance now uses the authored interaction/stand footprints, with a visible floor border and translucent fill during the relevant step.
  • FIX: Entering interaction range shows PRESS followed by the actual bound key and USE PRACTICE MACHINE. Successful use directs the player to MOVE TO PRACTICE POSITION; leaving range removes the use prompt.
  • FIX: Objective notifications and waypoint captions fit full gameplay text with bounded width, wrapping and sensible font sizing instead of ellipsis truncation. The minimap and compact HUD retain their layout.
  • IMPROVED: Practice groups now launch one second after all current targets resolve, with no overlapping groups or long fixed waiting timer. Pause freezes this delay.
  • FIX: The raised and widened authored PracticeLaunchOrigin is used as an oriented launch area. Singles vary, doubles launch from separated ends and triples from left/center/right positions.
  • PRESERVED: Ten singles, two doubles, two triples, 20 opportunities, 10/20 pass and retry, existing target arcs/hit-miss rules, Hounds, Throwers, gates and exact authored scene transforms.
