v0.1.99 - Temporary Chapter 1-5 skeleton

Save-first district transitions, temporary 50-Normal missions, a Core/Thrower placeholder finale and compatible Story suspension.

Temporary Offline Story route

  • NEW: The temporary Offline Chapter route now continues through Missions 1-3, 1-4 and 1-5 using the manually authored districts, checkpoints, entries, destination Marks and doors. These encounters are skeletons pending later redesign.
  • IMPROVED: Corridor checkpoint crossings save the next mission before its physical entry/title/encounter. Continue from a waiting checkpoint places the player safely just before the authored district entry; rear closure waits for whole-body doorway clearance.
  • NEW: Temporary Missions 1-3 and 1-4 each require 50 legitimate Normal kills from their own authored spawn regions. Their paired doors close on arrival and the forward route opens on secured completion.
  • NEW: Temporary Mission 1-5 keeps the authored visual Core and reuses four canonical Throwers and owned held/flying/landed Normals. Boundary 6 B closes as a single sliding wall using the authored gap; all Throwers and owned survivors must die.
  • NEW: Completed temporary 1-5 is saved before returning to Main Menu. Mission 1-6 stays locked; no Boss or final Core-defense/reward design is added.
  • PRESERVED: Three independent Story slots, existing 1-1/1-2 gameplay, restart/build data and read-only replay. Minimal 3/4/5 actor/gate/entry snapshots extend the existing suspend system, and physical floor-based minimap/waypoint handoffs retain the current styling. No adaptive pathfinding, Online or Endless changes.
