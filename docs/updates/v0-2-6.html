v0.2.6 - Chapter mission replay

Completed Chapter missions can replay fresh encounters while preserving furthest campaign progress and permanent profile.

Chapter mission replay

  • NEW: Completed Chapter Missions 1-1 through 1-5 remain marked COMPLETED and can be selected for REPLAY from NewMenu. Current missions continue normally; locked missions and Boss 1-6 remain unavailable.
  • IMPROVED: Replay starts a fresh selected-mission encounter using the current campaign build. Finishing, restarting or exiting replay preserves the furthest campaign progress, checkpoints and normal suspension; completed missions are not relocked.
  • PRESERVED: Replay uses the existing isolated, read-only session and Save & Exit safeguards. Permanent profile progression and neighboring Story saves remain untouched. No new objectives, rewards, balance changes or player-facing Story Reset controls.
