v0.1.95 - Three Offline Story campaigns

Three independent Story saves, last-played Continue, safe campaign migration and confirmed individual deletion.

Offline Story saves

  • NEW: Offline Story now opens Continue, New Save and Back. Continue resumes your most recently played campaign and is hidden when no campaign exists.
  • NEW: Keep up to three independent Story campaigns. Save cards show your role, mission, level and last-played time; empty saves begin through role selection.
  • IMPROVED: Existing Chapter progress migrates safely into Save 1, preserving missions, checkpoints, XP, cards, health, armor and role state. Each campaign progresses independently.
  • NEW: Delete individual Story saves with a confirmation dialog. Remaining campaigns, achievements, settings and Endless data stay intact.
  • PRESERVED: Completed-mission development replay remains read-only with zero progression. Tank and Healer role selection remains unchanged.
