v0.2.58 - Minimap facing cone and waypoint proximity

Show camera heading on the north-up minimap and hide redundant nearby destination waypoints.

Chapter navigation readability

  • MINIMAP: Added a subtle cyan camera-facing sector from the player marker. Its 64-unit radius fades toward the outer edge; angle follows horizontal camera FOV within a readable 50–100-degree range. The map stays north-up with unchanged District/corridor framing and marker sizes.
  • LAYERING: Map artwork draws below the facing cone, enemy/objective markers above it, and the player arrow above everything. The cone shares the existing camera-yaw source and remains clipped to the minimap frame without blocking input.
  • WAYPOINTS: Shared configurable three-meter suppression now covers Finalize Repairs and Chapter district destinations. Only floating world waypoint/distance hides nearby; objective/progress UI, minimap guidance and interactions remain active. Communications and D3 supply-area behavior are preserved.
  • PRESERVED: Per-waypoint radius overrides and opt-outs support future objectives. PracticeMachine interaction, practice-position and TARGETS cues remain visible nearby. The v0.2.56 CanvasRenderer fix, enemy markers, crosshair, pause, gameplay, saves and progression are unchanged.
