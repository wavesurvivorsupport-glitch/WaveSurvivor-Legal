v0.2.3 - DPS Mastery prototype and Story reset

A persistent direct-spend DPS Mastery prototype in NewMenu, with free respec and confirmed active-campaign Story reset.

DPS Mastery prototype

  • PROTOTYPE: NewMenu now includes a functional DPS Mastery tree: four sequential five-node branches, direct spending of 50 Mastery XP per node, persistent unlocks and a selected-node detail panel. All 20 nodes currently cost 1000 XP in total.
  • PROTOTYPE: Primary Attack, Dash, Micro-Grenade and Bullet Rain upgrades use configurable prototype values in the Offline Chapter DPS development flow. RESET MASTERY is free, refunds recorded node costs and preserves lifetime Mastery XP earned.
  • NEW: Confirmed RESET STORY on the NewMenu PLAY page restarts only the active Chapter campaign. Permanent profile progression, Mastery unlocks and other Story saves are preserved.
  • PRESERVED: The production MainMenu, LATEST, authored Chapter1_Blockout and build startup remain unchanged. Mission rewards, permanent Stats upgrades, final balance and Mission 1-6 remain future work.
