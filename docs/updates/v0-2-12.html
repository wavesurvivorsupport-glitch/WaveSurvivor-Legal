v0.2.12 - Chapter Normal HP and movement tuning

Chapter Normal enemies move at 4.5 and have 90 HP while damage remains 5. Fresh DPS and all other combat systems are unchanged.

Combat Balance Pass 1.2

  • TUNING: New Chapter 1 Normal enemies now use 90 HP and 4.5 movement speed, reduced from 100 HP and 4.8. Damage remains 5; attack range, cooldown and navigation are unchanged.
  • PRESERVED: Fresh DPS retains 10 primary damage, 3 shots/sec and 5 movement speed. Nine primary hits give an idealized 2.67-second kill time with the first shot at t=0. Hounds, Throwers, abilities, Stats, Mastery, encounter pacing and the close-range firing fix/muzzle/tracer presentation are unchanged. Balance Pass 1.2 awaits manual feel testing.
