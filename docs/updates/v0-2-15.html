v0.2.15 - District 1 completion handoff

Three authored corridor points separate durable first-clear rewards, middle-checkpoint results and existing District 2 entry. Prototype rewards are balance pending.

Chapter completion prototype

  • NEW: District 1 completion now uses the authored corridor finish, middle checkpoint and District 2 entry as separate handoff points. Successful finish immediately saves Mission 1 completion and unlocks Mission 2 before results appear.
  • PROTOTYPE — BALANCE PENDING: Mission 1's first clear grants 150 Credits and 100 DPS Mastery XP from centralized configuration. A profile-wide claim ledger and recoverable completion transaction prevent repeated grants through replay, restart, reload or interrupted saves. No Player XP reward is added.
  • NEW: The middle checkpoint shows DISTRICT 1 CLEARED with cosmetic earned → pre-balance → post-balance animations, CONTINUE and MAIN MENU. Continue restores control in the corridor; Main Menu returns to NewMenu and later Continue resumes the middle checkpoint.
  • PRESERVED: The authored District 2 entry keeps its existing title, safe rear-gate closure and arrival flow. Current communications, ambush, enemy balance, Stats/Mastery tuning, later missions, legacy scenes and production Build Settings remain intact.
