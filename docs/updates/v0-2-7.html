v0.2.7 - Chapter temporary cards retired

New Chapter 1 uses Permanent Stats and DPS Mastery, with safe retirement of run XP, temporary cards and obsolete card save restoration.

Chapter progression

  • CHANGED: NewMenu Chapter 1 gameplay now uses Permanent Stats and DPS Mastery. Temporary run XP/levels, card offers, Core card rewards and the card-only XP/level HUD are retired from this development path.
  • FIX: Chapter Continue, Restart and replay safely ignore obsolete selected cards and queued offers, including mission-start snapshots. Loading old card data alone does not rewrite the save or grant rewards.
  • PRESERVED: Permanent profile resources, Stats/Mastery effects and isolated replay remain intact. Legacy menus, LATEST, Endless and their card code/assets retain compatibility. Mission resource rewards are still future work.
