v0.2.8 - Chapter HUD cleanup

Retired run XP stays hidden and Chapter Armor appears only when live effective Armor capacity exists.

Chapter HUD

  • FIX: Chapter 1 hides the retired run/card XP bar, XP display and run-level badge during HUD setup and live refresh, including before combat bindings are ready.
  • IMPROVED: Chapter Armor HUD visibility follows live effective capacity. Zero capacity hides the entire bar; positive capacity remains visible when empty, such as 0/20. Runtime and temporary Armor changes refresh without a scene reload.
  • PRESERVED: Armor values, recharge delay, refill timing, Armor Recovery ranks and permanent progression remain unchanged. Legacy HUD paths and production scenes are preserved.
