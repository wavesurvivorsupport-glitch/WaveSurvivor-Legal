v0.2.66 · October 26, 2026

v0.2.66 - D1 final pressure and Primary balance

Slightly ease the final D1 repair tier, clarify survivor cleanup, widen Normal engagement and balance Primary Mastery and Lifesteal.

Chapter and DPS balance

  • D1 FINAL PRESSURE: The 40-50 Communications repair tier now spawns Normals every 1.15 seconds instead of 1 second. Earlier 4 / 3 / 2 / 1.5-second tiers, the ten-Normal opening, 5x5 repair zone, enemy stats and active cap remain unchanged.
  • D1 CLEANUP: Reaching 50 stops new Communications pressure. DEFEAT ENEMIES stays visible in the tracker while owned survivors or unpaid replacement slots remain; FINALIZE REPAIRS follows only after genuine cleanup. No survivors means immediate Finalize Repairs.
  • NORMAL RECYCLING: Engagement begins within 30 meters instead of 15. The same irreversible 20 gameplay-second timer continues outside range. Retirement grants no kill or XP credit; finite replacements start at full spawning-District HP, damage and speed with a fresh engagement clock (D3 80/80 HP; D5 100/100 HP). Hounds, Throwers and bosses remain excluded.
  • DPS PRIMARY MASTERY: Weapon Damage now grants +2 main Primary damage instead of +5. Twin/Triple auxiliary damage remains rounded 80% of starting base damage, excluding permanent AD and the Mastery damage bonus. Fire Rate nodes, projectile counts, base damage, permanent Stats and crit rules remain unchanged.
  • PRIMARY LIFESTEAL: Only the main projectile of each Primary attack contributes actual damage toward Lifesteal. Twin/Triple auxiliary projectiles keep their damage and crit rules but provide zero healing, including Enemy Tracker projectiles. Micro-Grenade, Bullet Rain and other damage-source healing remain unchanged.
