v0.2.9 - Chapter Rank-0 combat baseline

Prototype fresh DPS, Normal, Hound and stationary Thrower tuning, fixed Thrower cadence and a finite Bullet Rain tick budget. Pending manual feel testing.

Combat Balance Pass 1

  • PROTOTYPE: Chapter 1 fresh DPS now uses 100 HP, zero Armor, 5 movement speed and 10 primary damage at 3 shots/sec. Crit remains 0% with a 2x multiplier; Regen/Lifesteal remain zero. Dash cooldown is 5 seconds and fresh Micro-Grenade damage is 30 with a 10-second cooldown.
  • PROTOTYPE: Bullet Rain delivers exactly 12 base ticks of 5 damage across 6 seconds at 0.5-second intervals, with a 20-second cooldown. Its initial tick is included in the finite Chapter tick budget.
  • PROTOTYPE: Chapter Normal enemies use 100 HP / 5 speed / 5 damage; Hounds use 75 HP / 5.5 speed, plus 0.5 while flanking / 10 damage. Stationary Throwers use 500 HP, fixed 1-second preparation / 4-second reload and 30 melee damage; alive-count cadence is retired.
  • PRESERVED: Permanent Stats/Mastery tuning, authored ability geometry, mission populations, spawn pacing, Armor recovery and legacy combat assets remain unchanged. No progression-based enemy scaling is added. Balance Pass 1 is pending manual feel testing.
