v0.1.82 — DPS Aim Alignment and Progressive Repair Pressure

Primary shots follow the compact crosshair, while District 1 repair pressure increases through three configurable bands.

DPS Aim

  • Real primary shots now match the existing compact one-, two-, and three-projectile crosshair markers. Shared marker-to-camera aim drives muzzle convergence, with close-cover obstruction checks; weapon damage, speed, range, crit and projectile counts are unchanged.

District 1 Repair

  • DISTRICT 1: Repair enemy pressure now rises with progress: below 50 uses a four-second spawn interval and cap four; 50 to below 75 uses two seconds and cap five; 75 to below 100 uses one second and cap six. Thresholds, intervals and caps are configurable.
  • DISTRICT 1: Preserved authored spawn sources, six-unit player clearance, active-zone avoidance and legitimate enemy/XP ownership. Capped attempts do not queue bursts, and new pressure spawning still stops at 100 before Finalize Repairs. The established mission, zone, HUD, cards, minimap and gate flow remain unchanged.
