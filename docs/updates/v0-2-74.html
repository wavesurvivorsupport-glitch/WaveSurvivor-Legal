v0.2.74 - DPS effect audit and effective movement readout

Show temporary Momentum in offline current speed and verify Mastery and permanent Stat effects without changing combat balance.

DPS progression and feedback

  • CURRENT SPEED: Offline gameplay now shows the movement controller's effective speed, including temporary DPS Momentum. The buff itself remains +15% for 1.5 gameplay seconds after a completed Dash, refreshing without stacking.
  • ABILITY POWER CLARITY: Bullet Rain retains base 7 damage and its 0.2 AP coefficient. AP is rounded to whole bonus damage: AP 0-2 deals 7 per tick; AP 3-5 deals 8 before other modifiers. The Increased Damage Mastery makes those examples 8 and 9. Floating feedback continues to show actual applied damage.
  • EFFECT AUDIT: Focused checks verify all 20 DPS Mastery ownership connections, all 11 available permanent Stats, purchases, recovery-path exclusivity, respec and profile reload. No enemy, ability balance, Mastery price, progression gate or card tuning changes. Permanent Movement Speed remains removed.
