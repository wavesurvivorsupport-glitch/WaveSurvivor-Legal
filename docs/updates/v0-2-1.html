v0.2.1 - Persistent progression foundation

A shared permanent profile foundation for Player XP, Player Level, Credits and direct-spend DPS Mastery XP, plus retirement of DPS fire-rate stacking.

Progression foundation

  • FOUNDATION: Added shared permanent player-profile support for Player XP, Player Level, Credits and DPS Mastery XP, separate from the three Story campaigns.
  • CHANGED: Retired the DPS temporary stacking Fire Rate passive and its HUD tile. Normal primary fire and abilities retain their existing base behavior.
  • FOUNDATION: Credits and direct-spend DPS Mastery XP support safe save/load and spending. Mission rewards, Stats purchases and mastery nodes remain future work; Player Level currently grants no gameplay bonuses.
