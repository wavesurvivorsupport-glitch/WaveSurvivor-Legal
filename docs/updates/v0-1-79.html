v0.1.79 · October 5, 2026

v0.1.79 — Chapter 1-1 Playtest Polish

Larger player HUD, smaller tighter card choices, sealed gate backing and progressively revealed combat targets.

HUD and Cards

  • HUD: Compact Player HUD increased from 1.3 to 1.6. Armor and HP keep their width, use 0.9 height scale, and sit closely together; LEVEL UP now appears above the vitals.
  • CARDS: Bottom-left card choices now use a smaller 1.2 presentation with tighter spacing and fit beside the player HUD. Existing selection, animations, and queued offers remain intact.

Chapter 1-1

  • Reused the tested backing-wall arrangement to seal gaps around the closed gate while keeping its original opening animation and clear corridor passage.
  • Combat remains 10 → 10 → 20, but progress now reveals cumulative targets as 0/10 → 10/20 → 20/40 → 40/40. All 40 legitimate kills still open the exit.
