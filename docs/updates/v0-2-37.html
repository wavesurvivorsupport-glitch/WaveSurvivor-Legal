v0.2.37 - District floor arrival and encounter grace

Districts 3 through 5 now wait for actual floor arrival and three seconds of grace before encounter spawning, with saved grace and existing door safety preserved.

District encounter timing

  • FIX: District 3, 4 and 5 encounters no longer start their spawn timer at the entry trigger. Entry still activates and presents the District; actual District floor contact now arms a shared three-second grace period before the authored encounter begins.
  • FIX: Continue before floor arrival remains unarmed. Suspended floor-grace progress resumes from its saved timer, while already-started encounters restore their existing actors and phase without repeating the initial start. Legacy entry-based waiting time is not counted as floor grace.
  • PRESERVED: Rear doors still require actual floor contact, safe doorway clearance and being beyond the doorway. Combat grace is independent of door movement. District 2 entry, PracticeMachine and Hound/Thrower timing are unchanged.
  • PRESERVED: Authored triggers, CheckpointSpawn markers, floor sensors, doors and geometry are unchanged. Enemy counts/stats, Hound cadence, Thrower behavior, rewards, Credits/Mastery, map/minimap and player combat values are unchanged.
