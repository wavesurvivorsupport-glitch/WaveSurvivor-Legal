v0.2.28 - Clamped Chapter minimap camera

Heading-up minimap framing follows useful revealed map space, stops at boundaries and allows the player marker to move within the viewport.

Minimap framing rework

  • REWORK: Chapter heading-up minimap now uses a separate clamped map-camera center. It follows while useful revealed map space allows, stops at pan limits, and lets the player marker move within the viewport. The forced center/lower-center player anchor is retired.
  • FRAMING: Camera turns, zoom changes and discovery recalculate the legal view from revealed authored floor geometry. Smaller axes center their revealed region; bounded triangle-coverage checks prefer fuller useful-map views while keeping the player inside. Artwork and all markers share one camera, heading and scale.
  • PRESERVED: District/corridor reveal rules and hidden-area masking, Core proximity zoom and transitions, and current 34/20/24/30/28/36-pixel markers remain unchanged. Core/objective edge indicators now use the actual projected player position. No added zoom-out, gameplay, progression, geometry or non-minimap HUD changes.
