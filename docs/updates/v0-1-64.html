v0.1.64 — Tighter DPS Multi-Projectile Crosshair

The DPS primary-fire crosshair is more compact with two or three projectiles, while actual projectile spread stays the same.

DPS

  • Side crosshair markers for two- and three-projectile primary fire are positioned 40% closer to the screen center where their visual size allows.
  • The layout retains space between crosshair pieces at high FOV or narrow resolutions; projectile trajectories, damage, and Enemy Tracker behavior are unchanged.
