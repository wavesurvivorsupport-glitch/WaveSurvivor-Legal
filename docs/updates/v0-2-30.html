v0.2.30 · October 11, 2026

v0.2.30 - District 2 Hound pressure reduction

Fewer introductory Hounds and half-rate sustained Hound pressure, with individual Hound behavior and practice-target flight settings preserved.

District 2 pressure

  • TUNING: District 2 introductory Hound batches reduced from 1 / 3 / 3 / 5 to 1 / 2 / 2 / 3, totaling 8 instead of 12. The singleton encounter remains intact.
  • TUNING: Hound pressure during the four-Thrower encounter now spawns one every two seconds instead of one per second. The active cap remains disabled; individual Hound stats and behavior are unchanged.
  • INSPECTED, UNCHANGED: District 2 practice targets use a six-second flight duration for every single, double and triple launch. Actual travel speed depends on the endpoints and arc; no target speed or timing was changed.
  • PRESERVED: Opening Normal count, Thrower count/stats, target count, player balance, rewards, objectives and other encounter pressure remain unchanged. Existing suspended groups remain compatible.
