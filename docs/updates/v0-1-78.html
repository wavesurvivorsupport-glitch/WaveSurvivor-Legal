v0.1.78 — Chapter 1-1 First Playable Combat Slice

District 1-1 now plays as a real 10 → 10 → 20 combat sequence, then opens its authored corridor gate after 40 legitimate kills.

Chapter 1-1

  • Chapter1_Blockout starts at the authored District 1-1 marker with the original corridor exit sealed by two moving wall sections.
  • The real player weapon, canonical enemy damage/death pipeline, and normal XP remain active while three non-overlapping batches spawn from the authored District 1-1 regions: 10 enemies, then 10, then 20.
  • Only legitimate enemies owned by this encounter advance the continuous DEFEAT ENEMIES 0/40 objective. After 40/40, the two original gate walls smoothly return to their authored open transforms and the corridor is traversable.

HUD

  • The existing compact HUD, Chapter minimap framing, objective notifications, contextual event bar, card presentation, and other Story phases are preserved.
