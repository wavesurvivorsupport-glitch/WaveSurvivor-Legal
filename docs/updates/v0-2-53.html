v0.2.53 · October 24, 2026

v0.2.53 - District 2 final polish

Twenty opening Normals, a larger PracticeMachine interaction footprint, higher target arcs and shadow-free HP-bar graphics.

District 2 polish

  • D2 POLISH: Opening clear increases from 15 to 20 canonical Normals without changing enemy stats. The initial machine InteractZone grows from 1.5×1.5 to 2×2 meters; its center, vertical tolerance and later Practice Position remain unchanged.
  • TARGETS: Existing parabolic paths gain higher midpoint rises: single 3→10m, double 3.225→11m, triple 3.45/3/3.45→12/10/12m. Flight remains three seconds, with the existing endpoints, launch patterns, scoring, 2.5-second initial delay and one-second resolved-group pause.
  • HP BARS: Shared Offline/Online presentation disables shadow casting/receiving on health-bar mesh graphics only. Enemy body shadows, bar anchors, 1.5× width and 2× thickness remain intact.
  • PRESERVED: Machine/noise warning/four-Thrower sequence, v0.2.51 primed opening, carried-enemy targeting, Thrower payload pressure, no D2 Hounds, existing survivor completion, checkpoints, rewards and minimap.
