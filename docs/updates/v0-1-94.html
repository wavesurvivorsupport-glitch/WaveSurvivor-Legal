v0.1.94 - Story objective communication

Clear replay and role labels, temporary secured announcements, fixed survivor countdowns and authored District 3 navigation.

Story and mission UI

  • FIX: Mission role selection now reads SELECT ROLE with a fitted header. Completed-mission development replay uses the simple REPLAY label while retaining zero progression and read-only campaign protection.
  • IMPROVED: District 1 ambush guidance reads SURVIVE THE AMBUSH with the existing 40-enemy legitimate kill requirement.
  • FIX: DISTRICT 1 SECURED is a temporary announcement; afterward the real DISTRICT 2 objective and the original destination beacon return, including the Pause current objective.
  • FIX: District 2 cleanup snapshots owned survivors after the fourth Thrower dies and counts down against a fixed denominator. Zero survivors skip the cleanup bar; all legitimate survivors remain required.
  • FIX: DISTRICT 2 SECURED and PRACTICE COMPLETE use temporary feedback. After the secured announcement and gate opening, DISTRICT 3 navigation points to the exact authored Mark destination; Pause displays the real active objective.
  • PRESERVED: Authored transforms, repair and gate mechanics, Hound pressure, Thrower tuning, Practice gameplay, replay save protections and enemy speed 2.5. Mission 1-3 gameplay and its persistent transition remain future work.
