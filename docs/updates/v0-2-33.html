v0.2.33 - District 2 aim-trainer flight speed test

Practice target flight duration reduced from 6 to 4 seconds while initial delay, between-group cadence, sequencing and resolution gating remain unchanged.

PracticeMachine travel time

  • TUNING: District 2 aim-trainer targets now use a 4-second flight duration, reduced from 6 seconds. Single, double and triple groups share the same travel-time setting.
  • PRESERVED: The initial orientation delay remains 2.5 seconds. The next group still waits for all current targets to resolve, then waits 1.0 second; target counts, sequencing, scoring, hit/expiry rules, authored arcs and endpoints are unchanged. Unhit targets still expire on arrival. Existing suspended trajectories retain their captured duration; new launches use 4 seconds.
  • PRESERVED: District 2 enemies, combat balance, mission progression, rewards, checkpoints and minimap are unchanged. This is an isolated travel-time test.
