v0.2.72 - District 1 remaining-enemy progress bar

Keep the D1 cleanup count centered inside its existing objective track, with fill that advances through deaths, recycling and Continue.

District 1 cleanup HUD

  • D1 CLEANUP BAR: After Communications reaches 50/50, DEFEAT ENEMIES now shows its remaining count centered inside the existing objective progress track. The bar fills as the required survivors disappear, using the actual cleanup starting population rather than a fixed total.
  • D1 PROGRESS: Clearing three of ten starting survivors shows 7 REMAINING and 30% fill; clearing five shows 50%. Legitimate deaths and silent pressure-Normal recycling both advance cleanup. Recycling still grants no kill or XP credit, and no new Communications pressure spawns after 50.
  • D1 CONTINUE: Cleanup saves retain the original starting total alongside the current remaining actors, so Continue preserves fill and count. Older saves without a starting total derive a truthful baseline from their current required survivors. Pending finite obligations remain counted until genuinely resolved.
  • HUD SAFETY: Zero survivors proceeds normally to FINALIZE REPAIRS; an empty initial cleanup skips directly there. Repair Communications 0/50, the initial ten-enemy clear, other District bar layouts, objective guidance, crosshair, pause and the CanvasRenderer dependency remain unchanged.
