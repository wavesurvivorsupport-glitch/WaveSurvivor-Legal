v0.2.57 - District 3 chase escalation and ambush warning

A longer second supply chase and a saved gate-opening/red-warning finale add escalation while preserving the existing District 3 objective and combat composition.

District 3 feel and finale

  • D3: The second MOVE THE SUPPLIES chase at 40 progress now lasts 14 seconds instead of 10. The first chase at 20 remains 10 seconds; both retain 8 m/s speed and the continuing deterministic route.
  • D3 FINALE: Completing FINAL DELIVERY opens the authored Boundary 0 A/B arrival gates. A 1.75-second opening/hold beat leads into a 0.5-second established red WARNING pulse, followed by SURVIVE THE AMBUSH. The D4 progression gate remains sealed until D3 is secured.
  • SAVE / CONTINUE: Gate-beat and warning stages persist separately with their remaining timing. Captured gate motion resumes, warning progress continues, and existing ambush counters prevent duplicate activation. Older active ambush saves do not replay the anticipation.
  • PRESERVED: Opening 20 Normals + 5 Hounds, the 0/60 supply objective, pressure cadence and enemy stats remain unchanged. The finite final 15 Normals + 5 Hounds and all legitimate surviving owned enemies still must die before the existing secure, rewards, checkpoint, replay and D4 handoff flow.
