v0.1.90 — Mission 1-2: District 2

District 2 opening combat, Hound introduction, Practice Machine, four Throwers and a survivor-gated exit toward District 3.

Chapter 1 gameplay

  • NEW: Mission 1-2 is playable from the District 2 checkpoint: clear 10 Normals, then sequential Hound groups of 1, 3, 3 and 5.
  • NEW: The authored Practice Machine teaches single/double/triple arcs across 20 targets, passes at 10 hits and supports retry without restarting the mission.
  • NEW: Practice completion leads through noise warnings into four stationary Throwers at authored A/B/C/D positions, with independent health sections and real shootable held/airborne Normals that retain their HP.
  • NEW: Hound pressure uses a configurable five-second interval and cap eight without spawn backlog. All four Throwers and every owned survivor must die before District 2 is secured.
  • NEW: Boundary 3 closes at mission start and smoothly returns to its exact authored open positions after completion. A world marker points toward District 3; Mission 1-3 gameplay/checkpoint is not implemented.
  • PRESERVED: District 1 flow/checkpoint, compact HUD/minimap, normal weapons and authored enemy speed. DPS passive expiration freezes during downtime and remains active through survivor cleanup. Prototype encounter tuning awaits human playtesting.
