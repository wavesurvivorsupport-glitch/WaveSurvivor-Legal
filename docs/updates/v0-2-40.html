v0.2.40 - North-up minimap and player visibility

Chapter minimap remains north-up while the player arrow rotates with facing and draws above overlapping markers, preserving District and corridor framing.

Minimap orientation and player visibility

  • CHANGED: Chapter minimap is now north-up. Map artwork and enemy/Core/objective world positions stay fixed while the player turns. The player arrow rotates with camera facing: north up, east right, south down and west left.
  • FIX: The player marker has an explicit topmost presentation layer above enemies, Core, objectives and the Core edge indicator, so overlapping markers cannot hide the player arrow. Marker sizes and edge-indicator placement are unchanged.
  • PRESERVED: Active Districts frame themselves; transition corridors frame the previous District, corridor and destination; entry commitment reframes to the destination alone. Framing bounds, scale, smooth transitions, discovery and static settled centers are unchanged. Player-follow panning and Chapter Core zoom remain disabled. No combat, Chapter progression or Core gameplay changes.
