v0.2.54 - Communications objective marker readability

Hide redundant nearby waypoints and show the actual Communications zone beneath the player arrow.

Objective presentation

  • OBJECTIVE READABILITY: Communications world waypoint and distance hide inside its repair footprint or within three meters of its center at the objective height, and return when leaving. The objective tracker remains visible.
  • MINIMAP: Communications uses a thin square outline projected from its authored 5x5-meter objective footprint instead of an oversized point marker. The player arrow retains its explicit top layer; existing off-map guidance remains available.
  • REUSE: Shared waypoint presentation accepts an optional canonical zone footprint for future area objectives. Other markers, north-up orientation, District framing, objective gameplay, combat and spawns are unchanged.
