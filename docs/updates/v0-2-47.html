v0.2.47 - District 2 aim challenge and Thrower assault

A 15-Normal opening leads directly into the existing aim challenge and four-Thrower assault, with Hounds retired from District 2.

District 2 encounter identity

  • REDESIGN: District 2 now clears 15 canonical Normals, goes directly to the existing PracticeMachine aim challenge, then uses the TOO MUCH NOISE / ENEMIES ARE APPROACHING warning to begin its four-Thrower assault.
  • REMOVED FROM D2: Hound introduction groups, scent/breathing sequence and independent four-second Hound pressure. The assault has no independent extra Normal wave; only the Throwers themselves generate Normal payload pressure. Hound assets and AI remain available for later District 3 work.
  • PRESERVED: PracticeMachine keeps its 2.5-second initial delay, four-second target flight, single/double/triple sequence and one-second pause after each group resolves. Thrower stats/attacks/payloads and Normal/player balance are unchanged. All four Throwers and surviving owned Normals remain required before the existing secured, open-route, waypoint, checkpoint, results and reward flow.
  • COMPATIBILITY: Old Hound suspensions adapt in a detached runtime copy without kill credit or reward writes. Retired intro phases resume at the machine; assault/cleanup retain Throwers and required Normals while omitting Hounds. Unfinished older opening clears keep their actors/kills and add only the missing enemies needed for the new 15-Normal target. D1, D3+, minimap, progression and authored scene transforms are unchanged by this task.
