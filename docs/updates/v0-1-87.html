v0.1.87 — District 2 transition and checkpoint

District 1 now leads into a persistent District 2 checkpoint with a safe one-way boundary and restored Chapter progression.

Offline Chapter 1

  • NEW: After DISTRICT 1 SECURED, crossing the authored District 2 boundary safely activates an invisible one-way return wall once the player fully clears it.
  • NEW: District 2 entry marks Mission 1-1 complete, advances Chapter stage to 1-2 and saves the authored District2CheckpointSpawn through the existing Offline campaign system.
  • NEW: Continue and current-stage Restart restore District 2, the return blocker and the earned player build without replaying District 1.
  • PRESERVED: District 1 repair cleanup, ambush, lock recovery, HUD and authored standard enemy movement speed 2.5. District 2 gameplay is still to be designed.
