v0.2.14 - Chapter Normal HP tuning

Chapter Normal HP reduced to 80 while movement speed remains 4.5 and damage remains 5. Fresh DPS and all other combat and mission pacing remain unchanged.

Combat Balance Pass 1.3

  • TUNING: New Chapter 1 Normal enemy HP is reduced from 90 to 80. Movement speed remains 4.5 and damage remains 5; attack range, cooldown and navigation are unchanged.
  • PRESERVED: Fresh DPS remains 10 primary damage, 3 shots/sec and 5 movement speed. Eight landed primary hits give an idealized 2.33-second kill time with the first shot at t=0. Hounds, Throwers, spawn pacing/counts, abilities, Stats, Mastery, point-blank hit detection and tracer/muzzle presentation are unchanged.
