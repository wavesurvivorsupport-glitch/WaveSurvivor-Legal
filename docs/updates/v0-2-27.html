v0.2.27 - Forward-view minimap framing

The Chapter minimap gives more space ahead with a fixed lower-center player anchor, aligned artwork and markers, and preserved District reveal and Core zoom.

Minimap framing

  • IMPROVED: The Chapter heading-up minimap now places the player 15% of viewport height below center, giving more visible space ahead. The player remains horizontally centered and upright.
  • FIX: Artwork, enemies, Core and objectives share the same configurable lower-center anchor. Core and objective edge indicators follow rays from that anchor while staying inside the viewport.
  • PRESERVED: Camera-forward stays up, and the lower-center anchor remains fixed through Core zoom and corridor framing. Progression reveal masks still hide unreached Districts at every zoom level.
  • PRESERVED: Marker sizes remain 34 / 20 / 24 / 30 / 28 / 36 pixels. Authored artwork, world bounds, geometry, gameplay, objectives, rewards, checkpoints and other HUD elements are unchanged.
