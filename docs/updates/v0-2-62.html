v0.2.62 - District 5 Thrower suppression and reboot

D5 siege Throwers become persistent tactical threats: disable them temporarily, watch the warning, and preserve their state through resume.

D5 prototype lifecycle

  • D5 THROWERS: The four authored siege stations now shut down in place at zero HP instead of permanently dying. Inert bodies dim, stop attacking, and leave hostile targeting/minimap registries; collision, outline and health-bar presentation return on activation.
  • PROTOTYPE REBOOT: From Wave 2 onward, shutdown lasts 60 gameplay seconds with an orange warning during the final five. An eligible reboot restores full authored HP and the existing Normal-ready, one-second opening. Intermission expiry waits without creating a payload.
  • WAVE RULES: Wave 1 enables A/B while C/D remain dormant; disabled Wave-1 stations stay down. Wave 2 explicitly resets all four to full HP and primes them once. Final Wave preserves current HP, shutdown states and timers.
  • OWNERSHIP / REWARDS: Shutdown retires only uncommitted held preparation; released Normals remain legitimate enemies. Station shutdown never emits a death or kill/XP/achievement credit, preventing repeatable permanent-reward farming. Terminal siege states cancel reboot warnings and activation.
  • SUSPEND / ISOLATION: Per-slot lifecycle, HP, exact remaining time and phase permission restore alongside canonical preparation/payload ownership without extra priming. Legacy missing station bodies restore inert. D2 Throwers retain permanent death; siege quotas, Core HP, enemy balance and rewards are unchanged.
