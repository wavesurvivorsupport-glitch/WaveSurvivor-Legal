v0.2.46 · October 21, 2026

v0.2.46 - District 1 stationary Communications defense

District 1 becomes a stationary 0–50 hold with escalating Normal pressure, survivor cleanup and Finalize Repairs, replacing the moving phase and separate ambush.

District 1 encounter redesign

  • CHANGED: District 1 now centers on one stationary Communications defense after its existing 10-Normal opening clear. Repair remains 0–50 at one point per occupied second in the authored 5×5 area; leaving pauses repair but does not stop an already-started defense.
  • TUNING: Normal pressure escalates at 10/20/30/40 repair points using cadence only: one spawn opportunity every 4/3/2/1.5/1 seconds. The existing 15-active cap, safe shuffled spawn sources and 6-meter player clearance are preserved. Individual Normal HP, speed and damage are unchanged. These encounter values remain prototype tuning.
  • CHANGED: At 50 repair points, new defense spawning stops. Remaining owned enemies are cleared under DEFEAT ENEMIES, then the existing five-second Finalize Repairs interaction opens the forward gate and secures District 1. The separate post-repair ambush and lock overrides no longer run.
  • COMPATIBILITY: Old moving/ambush suspensions retain progress and owned survivors without creating new ambush waves. The authored stationary area replaces obsolete moving-zone positions; already-finished Finalize work is retained. Rewards, results, checkpoint authority, D1→D2 routing, minimap, economy and D2+ are unchanged by this redesign.
