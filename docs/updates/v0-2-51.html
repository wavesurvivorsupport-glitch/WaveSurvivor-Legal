v0.2.51 - Primed Throwers and persistent payload targeting

Throwers begin loaded with a one-second opening warning, and carried Normals remain targetable throughout their flight lifecycle.

Thrower combat presentation

  • COMBAT PRESENTATION: Fresh District 2 and District 5 Throwers appear with a Normal already loaded and release their opening throw after a one-second warning. Later throws retain the existing four-second reload and one-second preparation cadence.
  • FIX: Carried Normals remain independently owned enemies while following the Thrower hand anchor. Their existing health, hitboxes and silhouette renderer instances persist through carry, release, flight and landing; save data retains explicit held-owner identity.
  • FIX: Enemy Tracker acquisition and Tank/Healer primary queries now recognize carried payload trigger hitboxes. Other trigger behavior and existing Tracker range, cone, lock and wall-occlusion rules remain unchanged.
  • RESTORE: Suspended Throwers reconstruct captured state and timers without creating an extra opening payload or restarting the opening warning.
  • PRESERVED: Thrower counts/HP, Normal stats, Hounds, D5 Core pressure and Core HP, PracticeMachine, rewards, progression and weapon damage/rates/ranges are unchanged.
