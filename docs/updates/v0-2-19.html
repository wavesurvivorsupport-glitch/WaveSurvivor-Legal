v0.2.19 - Development full progression reset

A confirmed NewMenu testing reset restores a fresh profile and Chapter 1-1 while preserving preferences. Development tooling only.

Developer testing

  • DEVELOPER TOOLING: NewMenu now has RESET ALL PROGRESS beside QUIT in Unity Editor and Development Builds. A confirmation explains the full wipe; CANCEL or Escape dismisses it without changing progress.
  • RESET SCOPE: Confirmation resets Credits, Player XP/Level, available and lifetime DPS Mastery XP, all Mastery nodes, permanent Stats and recovery-path choice, local achievement progress and Chapter reward claims. All Story slots, checkpoints, suspension, replay and obsolete run/card state are cleared; fresh DPS Slot 1 starts at Mission 1-1 with later missions locked.
  • SAFETY: Owned progression files are archived, and a durable reset intent completes interrupted resets before saves are read. NewMenu refreshes without restarting. Graphics, audio, keybinds, FOV and other preferences are preserved; no external Steam achievement revocation is performed. Combat balance, normal Mastery respec, legacy menus and production Build Settings are unchanged.
