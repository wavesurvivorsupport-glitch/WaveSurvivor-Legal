v0.1.91 — District 2 entry and Hound introduction

Combat waits for real arena entry, rear walls close safely, checkpoint view faces forward, and Hound introduction uses one cumulative total.

District 2 playtest fixes

  • FIX: Mission 1-2 waits for the player to fully cross the authored District 2 arena-entry boundary; checkpoint spawning and corridor travel no longer start combat early.
  • NEW: The authored District 2 Boundary 4 A/B rear walls close symmetrically over 1.5 seconds after safe entry, preserving their exact saved open positions. Restart restores the rear gate open.
  • FIX: District 2 entry and Practice interaction/stand footprints are enabled invisible triggers and ignored by weapon queries. Checkpoint markers, return blocker and solid Practice barriers retain their intended roles.
  • FIX: Mission 1-2 checkpoint spawn resets camera pitch to level and faces the arena using authored spatial context, then resumes normal camera control.
  • IMPROVED: Clearing ten Normals gives a configurable 2.5-second SOMETHING HAS YOUR SCENT beat. Hound groups stay 1/3/3/5 with one continuous ELIMINATE HOUNDS counter from 0/12 to 12/12.
  • PRESERVED: Practice Machine firing, Thrower tuning, forward Boundary 3 exit, District 1, current HUD/minimap and authored geometry. Mission 1-3 remains unimplemented.
