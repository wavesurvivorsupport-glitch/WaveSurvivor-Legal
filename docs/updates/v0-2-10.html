v0.2.10 - Chapter Normal pursuit tuning

Chapter Normal movement reduced from 5.0 to 4.8 while retaining 100 HP, 5 damage and all other balance values.

Combat Balance Pass 1.1

  • TUNING: Chapter 1 Normal enemy movement speed is reduced from 5.0 to 4.8 for the next manual kiting test. HP remains 100 and damage remains 5.
  • PRESERVED: Player movement, Hounds, Throwers, weapons, abilities, spawn counts and encounter pacing are unchanged. Fresh DPS forward, backward and strafing all use the full 5-unit/sec movement limit; there is no backpedal penalty. Legacy enemy prefabs remain untouched.
