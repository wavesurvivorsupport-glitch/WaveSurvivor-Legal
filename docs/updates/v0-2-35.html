v0.2.35 - Static revealed-map minimap framing

Chapter minimap frames all revealed floors with a stable heading-safe scale, a moving player marker and no player-follow or Core proximity zoom.

Chapter minimap framing

  • CHANGED: Chapter 1 minimap now centers the entire currently revealed district/corridor floor area. The player marker moves across the static map; walking and camera heading no longer pan its world-space center.
  • CHANGED: A cached all-heading fit with a configurable 5% margin keeps revealed artwork visible during rotation. Hidden future floors do not affect framing. New reveals use the existing short smooth transition, then hold a fixed frame.
  • CHANGED: Chapter Core proximity zoom is disabled to preserve whole-revealed-map visibility. Player/enemy/Core/objective markers retain shared projection, heading-up presentation, existing sizes and useful edge indicators. The old player-follow/clamp/coverage solver no longer drives the Chapter minimap.
