v0.2.49 - Chapter floor glare reduction

Rougher Chapter district and corridor floors reduce specular glare while preserving wall response, blue lighting and reflections.

Floor material readability

  • MATERIALS: Generic Chapter district and corridor floors now use a rougher URP Lit response: smoothness 0.50 to 0.05. Metallic remains 0; specular highlights and environment reflections remain enabled, preserving softer local-light response while reducing sharp floor glare.
  • PRESERVED: Wall materials, blue navigation/PracticeMachine lighting, emissive surfaces, floor colors, shadow reception, Chapter lighting/fog/post-processing, geometry, gameplay and production Build Settings are unchanged. Only the four existing dedicated floor material assets were tuned.
