v0.1.71 — Contextual Event Progress and Timer Bar

Active Story requirements now share a compact top-center display with a persistent event icon, clear progress, and countdown direction.

HUD

  • Added one compact top-center contextual event bar with a persistent category icon, readable label, and current progress or remaining time.
  • Existing Explore kills and Core defense progress fill left to right; the existing Core travel timer drains right to left with an MM:SS countdown. Combat is red, defense yellow, and travel cyan.

Developer

  • Added shared event snapshots and optional Chapter 1 preview categories, including Boss countdown styling. Gameplay targets and timings, Boss HP, temporary objectives, the lower HUD, minimap, and card choices retain their existing systems.
