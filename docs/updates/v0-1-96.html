v0.1.96 - Mid-mission Story save and resume

Save & Exit preserves the current Story mission, enemies, Practice and player build together; Continue resumes the suspended session in its own save slot.

Offline Story persistence

  • FIX: Offline Story Save & Exit now preserves your current mission together with XP, cards, health, armor and role state. Continue resumes the suspended session instead of restarting it with advanced progression.
  • IMPROVED: Mission 1-1 resumes consumed opening kills, repair and cleanup progress, moving zones, ambush progress, lock recovery and gate state.
  • IMPROVED: Mission 1-2 resumes arena entry, Hound progress, Practice score and unresolved targets, surviving Thrower health, held/flying Normals and survivor cleanup.
  • FIX: Continue restores player position and view, current objectives and relevant combat HUD. Defeated enemies and resolved targets are not recreated or rewarded again.
  • PRESERVED: Suspensions belong to their individual Story save slots. Restart Current Stage intentionally discards the suspension and restores the canonical start/checkpoint. Existing saves remain compatible; development replay stays read-only with zero progression.
