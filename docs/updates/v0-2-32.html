v0.2.32 · October 14, 2026

v0.2.32 - Two-point Chapter checkpoint transitions

Authored checkpoints now own completion, rewards, saving and results; entry triggers start the next district, Continue resumes at entry and rear doors wait for actual floor arrival.

Chapter transition finalization

  • CHANGED: District transitions 1-to-2, 2-to-3, 3-to-4 and 4-to-5 now use two existing authored points. The next-district checkpoint completes the previous mission, grants eligible first-clear rewards, saves progression and opens its matching results panel. The entry trigger starts the next district. The old independent District1Finish step has no completion authority.
  • IMPROVED: Same-session CONTINUE restores control without teleporting. MAIN MENU / Continue and interrupted pre-entry sessions resume immediately before the current authored next-district entry, without repeating the prior panel, reward or checkpoint walk.
  • SAFEGUARDS: Rear doors wait for contact with the actual district Floor and safe doorway clearance, independently of entry-trigger activation. Checkpoint write failures pause traversal while retrying. Story schema 6 preserves old handoffs and journals; replay retains isolated continuation without canonical reward/save writes.
  • PRESERVED: Current authored scene objects, transforms, collider sizes and references remain unchanged. Existing first-clear reward values, combat, enemies, abilities, Stats/Mastery, mission encounters, minimap stability and production Build Settings are unchanged.
