v0.1.81 — District 1 Mission Flow: First Half

Clear the initial threat, repair communications through static and moving zones, and hold the final signal to open the gate.

District 1 Flow

  • CHAPTER 1: Replaced the earlier three-batch prototype with a District 1 intro, exactly ten legitimate initial kills, and a five-second post-clear beat before communications repair.
  • CHAPTER 1: Repair communications in a static 5×5 area from 0 to 50, then follow the same 3×3 area as it bounces within authored bounds from 50 to 100. Repair advances one point per second only while inside; leaving pauses progress.
  • CHAPTER 1: Authored District 1 enemy sources supply configurable repair pressure. At 100, new spawning stops and a five-second continuous hold at the final signal zone opens the existing animated gate. Surviving pressure enemies remain; ambush and door-lock recovery are future work.

Existing Systems

  • PRESENTATION: Reused existing objective notifications, world/minimap waypoints and contextual progress. Authored positions, HUD, cards, minimap framing, canonical combat and normal XP are preserved.
