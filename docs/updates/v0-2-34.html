v0.2.34 - District 2 sustained Hound pressure tuning

Post-machine Hound pressure reduced to one spawn opportunity every 4 seconds, preserving the 8-Hound introduction, enemy stats, four Throwers and all other District 2 pacing.

Post-machine Hound cadence

  • TUNING: District 2 post-machine sustained Hound pressure now attempts one Hound every 4.0 seconds, reduced from one every 2.0 seconds. This affects only the later Thrower encounter; phase start/end and unsafe-spawn discard behavior are unchanged. No spawn cap was added.
  • PRESERVED: Earlier Hound introduction batches remain 1 / 2 / 2 / 3, totaling 8. Hound HP, speed, flanking bonus, damage, attacks and AI are unchanged. The four Throwers, their Normal pressure, Normal values, PracticeMachine flight duration/cadence/counts, rewards, checkpoints, minimap and player combat are unchanged.
