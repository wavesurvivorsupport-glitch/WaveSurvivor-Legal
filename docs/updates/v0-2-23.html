v0.2.23 - Chapter heading-up minimap and larger markers

Camera look now points up on the Chapter minimap, with larger markers and preserved zoom, tracking and edge indicators.

Minimap readability

  • UI: Offline Chapter minimap now uses camera heading-up orientation. The map and world positions rotate around the existing projected player position; the player arrow remains upright and points toward the top. Camera look, not movement velocity, controls heading.
  • READABILITY: Chapter player arrow increased from 22 to 28 pixels and enemies from 12 to 16 pixels, with 20-pixel zoomed enemies. Core, objective and edge indicators grow by 25%; sizes are centralized in presentation configuration.
  • PRESERVED: Authored map artwork, world bounds, framing/zoom targets and transitions, enemy/Core tracking, and Core/objective edge-clamp paths remain intact. The minimap stays within its frame, icons remain readable, and legacy/online map settings and gameplay are unchanged.
