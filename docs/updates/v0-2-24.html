0.2.24 - Replay completion handoff

Chapter replay now reaches the District results panel with zero rewards and Continue / Main Menu choices, while preserving canonical progression.

Chapter replay

  • FIX: Mission 1 replay completion now reaches the normal District handoff instead of automatically returning to NewMenu.
  • IMPROVED: Replay results show DISTRICT 1 CLEARED, +0 Credits and +0 DPS Mastery XP with FIRST-CLEAR REWARD ALREADY CLAIMED. Permanent balances do not animate upward.
  • FIX: CONTINUE closes replay results and allows the authored corridor to District 2. At the next boundary, completed missions remain isolated replays; the legitimate current mission reloads its canonical checkpoint/session. MAIN MENU exits safely.
  • PRESERVED: First-clear rewards and their normal results animation are unchanged. Replay cannot farm rewards, overwrite later checkpoints or move canonical furthest progression backward.
