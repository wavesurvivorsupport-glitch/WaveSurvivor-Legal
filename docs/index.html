Build as you fight

Make the run yours.

Combat feeds a shared XP bar. When the team levels up, each player chooses upgrades for their own build.

Cards span rarities from Common to Legendary, creating room to strengthen your fundamentals or change how your role plays.

  • Team progress, individual choices
  • Build variety across runs
  • Decisions that matter alongside aim and teamwork

FAQ

Good to know.

More questions? Reach us through the support page.

How many players can play?

Online co-op supports one to three players. You can also play offline solo.

What modes are available?

Choose Story or Endless. Either content path can be played offline or online.

Do I need a separate game account?

No separate Haider Games account is required. Steam and online service requirements are explained in the Terms of Service.

Where are public patch notes?

The Updates page is the official source for WaveSurvivor patch notes.

Where can I report a bug?

Email wavesurvivor.support@gmail.com with what happened and the steps to reproduce it.
